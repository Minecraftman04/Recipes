Chocolate Brownies

Delia Smith American Brownies: rich chocolate brownies with optional chopped nuts, making 15 squares.

Makes
15 squares
Bake time
30 mins
Oven
180°C / 350°F / Gas 4
Tin
7 × 11 in / 18 × 28 cm

Before you start

  • Preheat the oven to 180°C / 350°F / Gas Mark 4.
  • Grease a 7 × 11 inch (18 × 28 cm) oblong tin and line it with greaseproof paper.

Ingredients

  • 110 g (4 oz) butter
  • 50 g (2 oz) plain dessert chocolate (unsweetened dark chocolate)
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 225 g (8 oz) granulated sugar
  • 50 g (2 oz) plain flour, sifted
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 110 g (4 oz) chopped nuts, optional — walnuts, almonds, hazelnuts, Brazil nuts, or a mixture

Method

  1. Melt the butter and chocolate, broken into small pieces, together in the top of a double saucepan. You can instead melt them carefully in a normal saucepan over gentle heat, taking care not to let the chocolate burn.
  2. Take the pan off the heat, then stir in all the remaining ingredients until thoroughly combined.
  3. Pour the mixture into the prepared tin and bake for about 30 minutes, until it begins to shrink away from the sides of the tin and the centre feels springy. Insert a knife into the middle; if it comes out clean, the brownies are ready. Check them every few minutes near the end of baking so they do not burn.
  4. Leave the brownies to cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then remove them and leave to cool completely on a wire rack.

Notes

  • The chopped nuts are optional.
  • Cut into 15 squares once cooled.
