8:3:1 Ginger Juice Shots

A punchy ginger-shot mixture made with an 8:3:1 ratio of apple juice, lemon juice and fresh ginger. The ginger is blended directly into the drink, so there is no juicing or straining.

Makes
About 1 litre
Prep time
5–10 mins
Ratio
8:3:1
Style
Strong / punchy

Ingredients

  • 85.8 g fresh ginger, peeled if preferred and roughly chopped
  • 690 ml apple juice
  • 260 ml lemon juice

The exact 8:3:1 scaling from 85.8 g ginger is 686.4 ml apple juice and 257.4 ml lemon juice; 690 ml and 260 ml are convenient kitchen measurements.

Method

  1. Add the 85.8 g ginger to a blender with about 200 ml of the apple juice.
  2. Blend until the ginger is broken down as finely and smoothly as possible.
  3. Add the remaining apple juice and all of the lemon juice.
  4. Blend again thoroughly until evenly combined.
  5. Pour into a clean bottle or jug and refrigerate. Shake or stir before serving because the blended ginger can settle.

Notes

  • Do not strain the mixture; the blended ginger stays in the drink.
  • The 8:3:1 version is intentionally strong and punchy.
  • For any batch size, use 8 parts apple juice, 3 parts lemon juice and 1 part ginger by the same scaling basis.
  • Serve in small shot-sized portions and keep refrigerated.
